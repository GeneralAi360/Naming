## 8. Evidence definitions


|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |

Required questions include:

- Does the name work before the explanatory story?
- Which association is audience-shared versus personal?
- What does the descriptor solve, and what does it merely hide?
- How is it different from the three closest competitor names?
- What is the likely spelling after one hearing?
- What happens when the portfolio expands?
- Which part of the success thesis depends on marketing execution?
- What legal, domain, or linguistic fact remains unresolved?
- What evidence would cause you to reverse your position?

### Adjudication order

1. validated knockouts;
2. unresolved high-severity evidence gaps;
3. strategic fit;
4. competitive distinction;
5. pronunciation, spelling, and memory;
6. cultural/linguistic fit;
7. domain and digital practicality;
8. preliminary trademark perspective;
9. architecture;
10. activation capacity and decision-owner commitment.

No majority vote overrides a validated knockout. One unsupported personal dislike cannot veto a candidate.
