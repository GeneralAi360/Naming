# 16. Mandatory limitations statement

> Naming is a strategic and creative decision under uncertainty. This report explains why certain candidates are more likely to support attention, recall, distinction, trust, word of mouth, and brand development under the approved conditions; it does not guarantee commercial success. Domain, company, app, handle, and trademark statuses can change. Preliminary trademark and linguistic screening do not replace qualified legal advice or native-speaker review. Re-check all volatile evidence before registration, filing, or public launch.
